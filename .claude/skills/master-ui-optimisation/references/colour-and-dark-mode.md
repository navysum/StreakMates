# Colour, Depth, and Theme Reference

## Product Colour Layers

### 1. Neutral Foundation
Canvas, surface, elevated surface, borders, primary/secondary/muted text.

### 2. Functional Accent
A ramp rather than one colour:
- primary
- hover
- pressed
- tint
- link
- selected
- focus

### 3. Semantic Colours
Success, warning, error, info, chart categories.

### 4. Theme
Adapt lightness, chroma, contrast, and surface depth for light/dark/custom themes.

## Dark Mode
Do not invert.
- elevated surfaces can become slightly lighter
- borders often need less contrast
- shadows become less important
- accent chroma/brightness may need tuning

## Data
Colour meaning must be stable and understandable without relying on colour alone.

## Supersession
Do not force a 60/30/10 percentage split onto complex product UI.
